# PixelFlow privacy policy

_Last updated: October 17, 2026_

PixelFlow ("the app") is an automation app for Android that runs routines you create, such as changing your phone's settings when you connect to a Wi-Fi network or at a set time. This policy explains what information the app uses and what happens to it.

**In short: PixelFlow has no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and it does not send your information to us or to anyone else. Everything the app uses stays on your device.**

## Information the app uses

PixelFlow only uses the information below to run the routines you set up, and only on your device:

| Information | Why it's used |
|---|---|
| **Location** (precise, including in the background, if you allow it) | To run routines when you arrive at or leave a place, and to calculate sunrise and sunset times for your area. |
| **Wi-Fi network name** | To run routines when you connect to or disconnect from a network you chose. |
| **Bluetooth device names** | To run routines when a device you chose connects or disconnects. |
| **App usage** (which app is open, if you grant usage access) | To run "App opened" routines. Only the name of the app on screen is checked; nothing is recorded or kept beyond what's needed to notice a change. |
| **Battery level and charging state** | To run battery and charging routines. |
| **Your routines and their run history** | Stored in the app so your routines work and you can see when they ran. |

The app also changes system settings you choose (for example ringer mode, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, dark theme or Always-On Display), using Android's own controls and, if you set it up, the Shizuku app on your device.

## What is shared

PixelFlow does not collect, sell or share your information, and it does not send data to the developer or to third parties. The only network access in the app comes from Google Play's purchase and review services described below.

Some features rely on services built into your phone, which are covered by their own privacy policies:

- **Google Play** handles optional support purchases and in-app review requests. Payments are processed entirely by Google; PixelFlow only learns whether you are a supporter. See the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
- **Android location services** may be used to find your current position or turn coordinates into a place name, as provided by your device.
- **Android backup**: if backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may include your PixelFlow routines in your device backup (for example to your Google account), so they can be restored on a new phone. You control this in your phone's settings.

## Storage and deletion

All PixelFlow data is stored on your device. You can delete it at any time by deleting routines in the app, clearing the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstalling the app.

## Permissions

You can grant or revoke PixelFlow's permissions (location, notifications, Bluetooth, usage access, alarms and reminders, Do Not Disturb access) at any time in Android settings. Routines that need a revoked permission will stop working until it is granted again.

## Children

PixelFlow is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
